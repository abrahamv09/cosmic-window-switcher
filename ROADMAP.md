# COSMIC Window Switcher Roadmap

## Version 1

Deliver fast, predictable Window switching through MRU Order and Live
Thumbnails. The Switcher Grid selects and activates Windows without duplicating
COSMIC's workspace-management interface.

- Include application Windows from every workspace in one MRU Order and follow
  a selected Window to its existing workspace through normal COSMIC
  activation. Workspace views, targets, and Window relocation remain owned by
  COSMIC and are not part of this app.

## Version 2

- Close a Window from its Switcher Item.
- Minimize or restore a Window from its Switcher Item.
